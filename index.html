(01) About

I’m Anubhav Gain (mranv), a security engineer at Infopercept in Mumbai, India. I build the controls that decide what an endpoint may run, detection pipelines that turn alerts into answers and Rust tooling that survives production, for lenders, insurers and SOC teams across India.

Since July 2024 I have worked at Infopercept as a security software engineer, building the Invinsense XDR deployment architecture and Invinsense Unified Endpoint Control. I have also consulted on endpoint security at Mahindra Finance and application security at SBI Life. Before that I was a DevSecOps engineer at Atcults (November 2023 to July 2024) and an IT security specialist at Parul University (November 2022 to October 2023). I hold a B.Tech in Cyber/Computer Forensics and Counterterrorism from Parul University (2021–2025) and two 2023 licentiates from Charles Sturt University, in Ransomware Techniques and Cybersecurity Management. My 80+ verified Credly badges include the IBM Cybersecurity Analyst and Google Cybersecurity professional certificates. I maintain open-source Rust and Go tools, including eBPF File Monitor, Krustron and Agni, and I founded Gain Fisheries, a fish hatchery in Pakhanjur, Chhattisgarh, in 2020.
